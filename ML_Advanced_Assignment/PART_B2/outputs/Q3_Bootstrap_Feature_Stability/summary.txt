Q3 — Bootstrap feature-selection stability
==========================================

Bootstrap resamples: 80; each sample is drawn with replacement and contains 90 rows.
Lasso alpha fixed at 0.08 to make selection frequencies comparable across resamples.
Known signal features selected in at least 70% of resamples: gene_141, gene_223, gene_3, gene_8, gene_16, gene_66.
Mean selection frequency for known signal features: 0.634.
Mean selection frequency for noise features: 0.151.
High selection frequency suggests robustness to changes in sampled observations. Correlated predictors can substitute for each other, so individual feature stability may be low even when a group carries signal.
Stability is not proof of causality.
